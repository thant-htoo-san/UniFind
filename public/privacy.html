UniFind Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

UniFind helps campus users post and discover lost and found items. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Account details (email, display name) to create and manage your account.
  • Item content you submit (title, description, location, and photos).
  • Messages you send in-app to coordinate returns.
  • Basic device and app diagnostics to keep the app running.

Camera and Photos

We request camera or photo library access only when you choose to upload item images. Photos are used solely for the content you submit in the app.

How We Use Data

  • To display posts and messages within UniFind.
  • To improve app reliability and fix bugs.
  • To respond to support requests.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Content you post (items, images, and messages) is visible to other UniFind users as part of the service.

Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated content by contacting us.

Your Choices

  • Update or delete your posts from within the app.
  • Contact us to delete your account and associated data.
